Hi, I’m Umut! I am a Master’s student in [Data Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/study/programmes/master/data-science.html) at [Saarland University](https://www.uni-saarland.de/) and a research assistant in the [Computer Vision and Machine Learning (D2) department](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/computer-vision-and-machine-learning) at the [Max Planck Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/home) in Saarbrücken, Germany. My research interests lie at the intersection of **computer vision, representation learning, generative modeling, and robot learning**, with a particular interest in learning visual representations for robotic manipulation.

Before moving to Germany, I received my B.Sc. in **Computer Engineering** from [Eskişehir Technical University](https://www.eskisehir.edu.tr/en). During my undergraduate studies, I worked on computer vision and deep learning, particularly **semi-supervised learning and medical image analysis**.
